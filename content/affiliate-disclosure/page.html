AIRecorderGuide.com uses the Amazon Associates tracking ID airecgd-20 on marked Amazon links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Manufacturer citation links and official-site links are not automatically Amazon affiliate links.

Paid Amazon links are labeled at the point of click. We do not copy Amazon star ratings, review counts, live prices, stock claims, or deal claims into our editorial cards unless that data is supplied through an Amazon-approved API or linking tool.

Mechanics

How affiliate links work here

The mechanism is straightforward: you click a marked Amazon purchase link, land on Amazon, and if your visit results in a qualifying purchase under Amazon's rules, Amazon may pay us an advertising fee. The product costs you the same either way — the commission comes out of the retailer's margin.

That revenue helps fund the research while keeping the commercial relationship with the retailer rather than selling rankings to manufacturers. The full business-model picture is in about us; the guardrails are the rest of this page.

The firewall

Why commissions can't touch rankings, picks, or the TCO math

Three structural reasons, not promises:

  1. 01The rankings follow published arithmetic.

    Our methodology weights five criteria — total cost of ownership ~30%, transcription economics ~25%, capture reliability ~20%, software maturity ~15%, company trust ~10% — and the TCO figures are hardware price plus three years of the cheapest covering tier, verified August 2026. A commission can't change arithmetic without the numbers changing in public.

  2. 02Our top picks would be terrible affiliate plays.

    We rank a $99–169 subscription-free device (HiDock, Pocket, iFLYTEK) as the answer to subscription fatigue, and we criticize the market leader's $239.99/yr tier in detail. A commission-optimized site optimizes for expensive hardware with recurring plans. Read ours and you'll see the opposite bias: spend less.

  3. 03Editorial outranks revenue, in writing.

    The editorial policy separates editorial decisions from any commercial relationships, and corrections from readers — or manufacturers — flow through the same verification queue. If a revenue-adjacent claim can't survive sourcing, it doesn't publish.

Hard lines

What we would never do

  • Paid placements. No manufacturer can buy a position, a review, or inclusion in a guide. Picks come from the methodology, full stop.
  • “Best product” labels for sale. Awards and “Best for X” tags reflect our criteria, never a fee. If a label ever has a commercial arrangement behind it, you'll see it in neon on this page.
  • Hiding the money. No unmarked affiliate links, no link cloaking, no “we may earn a commission” buried where you won't read it. Disclosure lives at the click, and this page stays current.
  • Softening criticism for a partner. Our device database carries negative trust notes, conflicting-spec flags, and owner complaints — including for the products most likely to be affiliate partners someday. That never changes.

Regulation

FTC compliance intent

Our disclosure practices are built to comply with the intent of the FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255): a clear, conspicuous disclosure of material connections, placed where the endorsement is encountered — not a footnote. Amazon purchase links carry rel="sponsored" attributes and a visible paid-link disclosure at the CTA.

Questions or challenges about any of this go through the contact form — including “I think this link earns and isn't disclosed properly,” which we treat as a correction-priority report under our editorial policy.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this site currently earn affiliate commissions?
It can. AI Recorder Guide participates in Amazon Associates, and marked Amazon links can earn us advertising fees from qualifying purchases. The Amazon disclosure appears at the link and in the site footer.
If commissions start, will prices change for me?
No. Affiliate commissions are paid out of the retailer's margin — the product costs you the same whether you click our button, go direct, or search for the store yourself.
How would I know if a link earns a commission?
Every Amazon affiliate CTA is labeled as a paid link directly at the button, and this page's status section carries the current state, dated.
