The short answer

Most buyers never need a paid tier — buy on hardware, then let your minute count decide

If you transcribe under ~5 hours of conversation a month, the free tier of nearly any device covers you — and the right purchase decision is entirely about hardware and form factor. Past that line, value diverges sharply: TicNote Pro at $79/yr for 2,100 min/mo is the cheapest ladder in the category [SPEC], while “Unlimited” tiers from PLAUD, soundcore, and Mobvoi have quietly converged on the same $239.99/yr price [SPEC]. And a small group of devices — HiDock P1, Pocket, Omi, iFLYTEK — sidestep the meter entirely.

Read the market

Four patterns hiding in the pricing

1. 300 free minutes is the emerging industry standard

PLAUD Starter, soundcore Starter, and SwitchBot's announced MindClip allowance all land on exactly 300 min/mo [SPEC]. That's 5 hours — deliberately enough to feel generous in month one and tight by month two for anyone recording weekly. When three competitors pick the same number, that's market coordination, not coincidence.

2. “Unlimited” converges at $239.99/yr

PLAUD Unlimited, soundcore Unlimited, and Mobvoi TicNote Unlimited all cost $239.99/yr [SPEC]. Android Police looked at the CES 2026 wave of note-taking wearables and called it “a shameless money grab” [REVIEW] — a blunt take, but the price-matching speaks for itself. Note that PLAUD's “Unlimited” still carries a 24-hours-per-day processing cap [SPEC].

3. The outliers define the value ladder

Mobvoi's 600 free min/mo is the generous outlier — earlier TicNote units shipped with 300 and the allowance was raised [SPEC]. Fieldy's 150 min/mo is the stingiest [SPEC]. And the four model breakers above simply refuse the ladder. Between those poles, the paid-tier value ranking is stable: TicNote Pro ($79/yr, 2,100 min) > Notta Pro ($97.99/yr, 1,800 min) > PLAUD/soundcore Pro ($99.99/yr, 1,200 min) [SPEC].

4. The hooks are always the same two features

Read any tier ladder and the paid hooks repeat: speaker labels/diarization and GPT-style summaries & Q&A. Pocket gates auto speaker-naming behind Premium; soundcore and PLAUD gate summary models behind Pro [SPEC]. If you only need raw transcripts, you can often stay free longer than the pricing page implies — see our diarization guide for what those features actually do.

The leader's details

PLAUD's fine print: add-on packs and caps

PLAUD is the reference implementation of this pricing model, so its details matter to everyone (rivals copy them). Beyond the three headline tiers — Starter (free, 300 min/mo), Pro ($99.99/yr or $17.99/mo, 1,200 min/mo), Unlimited ($239.99/yr or $29.99/mo) [SPEC] — three fine-print items from PLAUD's own support pages:

  • Add-on minute packs: $59.99 for 3,000 minutes or $99.99 for 6,000 minutes, each valid for 24 months from activation [SPEC] — a middle path between upgrading and waiting out the month.
  • Per-file cap: every user is limited to transcribing single files of no more than 5 hours [SPEC] — relevant to conferences, all-day events, and back-to-back lectures recorded as one file.
  • “Unlimited” caps processing at 24 hours per day [SPEC] — a legal-ese ceiling you'll never hit, but it's why the tier isn't labeled “infinite.”

Unverified claims we've seen and don't repeat as fact: a “Large” $24.99/4,000-min tier (third-party blog only) and “400 free minutes from year two” (single source). Neither appears on plaud.ai's own pages as of August 2026. The whole PLAUD economics story — with the per-minute math — continues in the alternatives guide and PLAUD vs Pocket.

Read this twice

The #1 confusion: recording is not transcription

The single most recurring question across PLAUD Facebook groups and owner threads, asked nearly verbatim every week:

“Are transcription minutes required for recording over 300 minutes?”
— Facebook, PLAUD community

No — and this applies to every device in this guide. Recording is a local, on-device function with no meter anywhere in the market: your 64 GB of storage holds hundreds of hours regardless of plan. What's metered is the AI transcription of those recordings in the vendor's cloud. Run out of minutes and your recordings still exist — they just sit untranscribed until next month, an upgrade, or an add-on pack.

The practical consequences: you can record conservatively and batch-transcribe only what matters (many free-tier owners do exactly that); deleting unwanted recordings before they're ever transcribed costs nothing; and buying hardware “because it records” never requires a subscription — the sub is purely a transcription-and-AI purchase. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a subscription to use an AI voice recorder?
No, not for any major device. Recording is always unmetered; a free tier (300 min/mo at PLAUD, soundcore, SwitchBot; 600 at TicNote; 120 at Notta) handles transcription for light users; and several devices — HiDock P1, Pocket (claimed), Omi's on-phone mode, iFLYTEK — skip or effectively skip the meter entirely.
What happens if I go over 300 minutes on PLAUD?
Recording continues unlimited — only AI transcription pauses until next month. To keep transcribing: upgrade to Pro (1,200 min/mo) or Unlimited, or buy an add-on pack (3,000 min for $59.99 or 6,000 min for $99.99, valid 24 months). Note the separate 5-hour per-file transcription cap.
Does recording use transcription minutes?
No. Minutes are consumed only when audio is sent for cloud transcription and summarization — never by the act of recording. This is the #1 confusion in owner communities, and it's true across every device we track, not just PLAUD.
Is PLAUD's subscription worth it?
Depends entirely on volume. Typical undergrads (5–10 hrs/month) fit inside free or Pro tiers; heavy lecture schedules (~40 hrs/month) exceed even Pro's 20 hours and push toward Unlimited. Owner sentiment splits the same way — some call the price 'egregious,' others treat it as core infrastructure. Run the minutes calculator before paying for any tier.
Which AI recorder has the cheapest subscription?
Among metered brands: Mobvoi TicNote — 600 free minutes/month and Pro at $79/yr for 2,100 min/mo, the best paid-tier value in the category. Among unmetered: HiDock P1 (unlimited included, $169 total) and iFLYTEK (unlimited offline, $0 subscription ever) — though each trades away some English accuracy or polish versus the cloud leaders.
Why do 'Unlimited' tiers all cost $239.99?
PLAUD, soundcore, and Mobvoi all price their top tier at $239.99/yr — price convergence that suggests the market is coordinating around a psychological ceiling rather than competing on value. Android Police called the CES 2026 note-taker wave 'a shameless money grab.' Our answer: compute whether you'd actually transcribe more than 1,200 minutes a month before considering any Unlimited tier — most buyers never do.
