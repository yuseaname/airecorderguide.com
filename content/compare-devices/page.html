Strengths & trade-offs

PLAUD Note

Strengths

  • Thinnest card format in the category — genuinely wallet-able
  • Best-known call-recording workaround for iPhone via the magnetic case
  • Mature app: speaker labels, summaries, Notion + ChatGPT export
  • Recording itself is unlimited — only transcription is metered

Trade-offs

  • Only 300 free transcription minutes/month; Unlimited costs $239.99/yr
  • Cloud-only transcription — no offline option
  • Owner reports of speaker-label errors and billing/support friction on Reddit
  • 5-hour per-file transcription cap

⚠ Trustpilot shows 4.6/5 (~899 reviews, as of August 2026), but Reddit contains recurring complaints about subscription value, speaker labels, and support. Weigh both.

HiDock P1

Strengths

  • Strongest no-subscription value: unlimited transcription for life of device
  • Unique Bluetooth-earphone method captures both call sides
  • $169 total 3-year cost — cheapest credible card path

Trade-offs

  • Battery/storage specs not clearly published
  • Smaller brand than PLAUD/Anker
  • Hardware design is bulkier than the ultra-thin cards

TicNote

Strengths

  • 600 free minutes/month covers many students and light users entirely
  • Live on-screen transcription while recording (PLAUD processes after)
  • Pro at $79/yr for 2,100 min/mo is the cheapest paid tier in our dataset
  • ZDNet praised its travel/workflow utility

Trade-offs

  • Brand presence and US retail availability trail PLAUD
  • BGR questions subscription value for light users
  • Earlier units shipped with a smaller 300-min free tier

Reading the table

What each column means

Street price*
Typical observed price range, editorial research dated August 2026 — not a live price. Verify on Amazon before buying.
Form factor
Card (phone-mounted), wearable (pin/pendant), coin, ring, wristband, or handheld — how the device travels with you.
Free transcription/mo
Monthly transcription minutes included before a paid plan is required. Recording itself is usually unlimited; transcription is what gets metered.
Transcription mode
Where audio is processed: cloud (uploaded), hybrid (on-device plus cloud features), or local (on-device only — the strongest privacy posture).
Phone calls
Yes = true call capture; “placement only” = captures calls only when held against the phone speaker; No = not supported.
Battery
Manufacturer-specified continuous recording or standby figures, labeled [SPEC]; owner-reported figures are labeled [REVIEW].
Storage
On-board recording capacity before sync is required.
Cheapest paid tier/yr
The lowest-cost subscription that unlocks full transcription, annualized — $0 means a usable free tier exists.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does “placement only” mean for phone calls?
Some card-style devices capture calls only when physically held or mounted against your phone’s speaker (for example, via a magnetic case). That is useful but different from true call capture, where the device records the call itself — the table marks the difference so you do not buy the wrong tool for phone interviews.
How is the 3-year cost column calculated?
Hardware price plus three years at the cheapest available tier — if a device offers a usable free tier, the plan cost is zero and the 3-year total equals the hardware price. The full method, with worked examples, is in our TCO study.
Why is the discontinued Limitless Pendant missing?
The comparison covers the 16 devices you can buy today. The Limitless Pendant was discontinued in December 2025 and now serves as our editorial case study on cloud-dependent hardware risk — you will find it in the rankings, not in purchasing tools.
