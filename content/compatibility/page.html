“Does it work with my phone?” is three questions wearing one coat — and whether you need a device beyond your phone is a fourth: does the companion app run on your platform, can the device capture the thing you record most (calls, meetings, lectures), and where do the outputs go (desktop, Notion, ChatGPT). The AI recorder market is phone-first by design, so the first question is nearly always “yes” — it's the second and third where devices genuinely differ.

Everything below comes from manufacturer pages [SPEC] and owner reports [OWNER], verified August 2026. App support shifts with app updates — treat this as the map, and the linked detail pages as the terrain.

The short answer

Every major recorder pairs with both iPhone and Android — real compatibility lives in call capture, live transcription, and desktop

All six devices below ship iOS and Android apps [SPEC]; the iFLYTEK SR302 Pro goes further and doesn't need a phone at all. The differences that actually change your day: phone-call capture (PLAUD's vibration-sensor case, HiDock's Bluetooth earphones, Notta's bone-conduction mic), live transcription (TicNote transcribes while recording; PLAUD processes after), and desktop — where PLAUD launched a meeting-notepad app alongside the NotePin S [SPEC], and most rivals offer a web app or nothing.

Best iPhone story: soundcore Work — MFi-certified [SPEC]

Best desktop story: PLAUD — desktop app + web app [SPEC]

No phone needed: iFLYTEK SR302 Pro — standalone with a screen

The compatibility matrix

Two things this matrix can't show

First, app quality varies by platform: PLAUD's Android support articles dominate its help center, and newer app versions dropped older Android releases — the pairing-fix detail guide covers this. Second, specs age fast: PLAUD added a desktop meeting app only in January 2026 [SPEC]; expect this table to keep moving. Also on the horizon: SwitchBot's announced MindClip lists real-time transcription in its support FAQ [SPEC], but has no release date.

Omi deserves a row of prose even though it's off-matrix: apps exist for iPhone, Android, Mac, and any browser [SPEC] — the widest platform spread in the category, a perk of its open-source app ecosystem.

Detail guide 1

Phones: iPhone & Android

The mobile story splits cleanly: iPhone buyers should care about call capture (iOS restricts call-recording apps, so hardware tricks like PLAUD's MagSafe vibration sensor matter) and MFi certification — soundcore markets the Work as “The Only MFi-Certified AI Voice Recorder for iPhones” [SPEC], and Vibe's Dot mounts via MagSafe with an accessory ring for Android [SPEC]. Android buyers inherit the market's most documented pain point: Bluetooth pairing, documented across a stack of PLAUD support articles and owner threads. Every fix, by device, is in the iPhone & Android guide, with the troubleshooting ladder in setup & troubleshooting.

Detail guide 2

Mac & Windows: the desktop landscape

Desktop is the category's weakest flank, and honesty is the only useful policy here. The bright spots: PLAUD launched a desktop meeting-notetaker app alongside the NotePin S at CES 2026 [SPEC]; PLAUD and Notta both run web apps [SPEC]; HiDock sells the H1 Lite ($189) explicitly as an “AI audio companion for Mac mini and modern desktops” [SPEC]; the iFLYTEK recorders work fully standalone with file transfer to a PC; and Omi ships a native Mac app [SPEC]. The catch: most of these devices are phone-first companions, and desktop workflows are largely export/import-shaped. The full picture — including what “works on desktop” really means per device — is in the Mac & Windows guide.

Detail guide 3

Zoom, Teams & Meet

The question Facebook PLAUD groups ask most: “Is there a way to have it interface directly to Teams?” The honest answer is no — no hardware recorder joins a meeting as a participant. But there are three working methods: room-audio capture near your speakers, call-recording mode against the machine, and PLAUD's desktop app with Bluetooth headsets, which owner reports say handles Zoom and Teams meetings successfully [OWNER]. Software meeting bots (Otter and friends) are a different tool for a different job. The comparison lives in the Zoom/Teams/Meet guide, with meeting hardware picks in the meetings guide.

Detail guide 4

Exports & integrations

Where transcripts end up is now a real differentiator. PLAUD has the deepest surface: native Notion export, Zapier automations, Dropbox workflows, and an MCP server that lets ChatGPT pull transcripts directly [SPEC, corroborated by owner reports]. Notta's Business tier adds Salesforce and Google Calendar; Vocci advertises ChatGPT/Claude integrations; Omi is open-source with an SDK for anything you can code. Transcript export options (timestamps, speaker labels) and the build-your-own-workflow path are covered in the exports & integrations guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do AI voice recorders work with both iPhone and Android?
Yes — every major device we track ships both iOS and Android companion apps, and they pair over Bluetooth. The platform differences that matter: call capture is a hardware question on iPhone (iOS blocks call-recording apps), and Android users see more documented Bluetooth-pairing issues, especially on older OS versions with newer app releases.
Why won't my PLAUD connect to the app?
The fix ladder from PLAUD's own support docs: update the app, check Bluetooth and Location permissions, restart the phone, and hold the record button 12–15 seconds to reset the device. Also confirm your Android version is still supported — newer PLAUD app versions dropped older Android releases. Our setup & troubleshooting guide walks every step by device.
Can I use an AI recorder without a phone at all?
One mainstream option: the iFLYTEK SR302 Pro transcribes on-device with its own screen and no account, then transfers files to a PC. Every other device records standalone (capture never needs the phone) but needs the companion app — on phone, desktop, or browser — for transcription and summaries. Omi covers the most platforms: iOS, Android, Mac, and browser.
Can I record Zoom or Teams meetings with an AI recorder?
Yes, three ways: place the recorder near your computer speakers (room-audio capture), use call-recording mode with the sensor against the machine, or use PLAUD's desktop app with Bluetooth headsets, which owner reports say handles Zoom and Teams. The recorder never appears as a meeting participant — that's software-bot territory. Details in the meetings-app guide.
Can I export transcripts to Notion, Obsidian, or ChatGPT?
PLAUD: yes natively — Notion export in the app, Zapier automations, Dropbox workflows, and an MCP server that lets ChatGPT pull transcripts directly. Others export files you route yourself (our recommended export-to-Obsidian-to-LLM workflow is in the exports guide). Exact PLAUD file-format lists should be confirmed on the support portal before you build a pipeline on them.
