FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly do you respond?
Corrections are the priority queue — verified errors are fixed within five business days with a note, per our editorial policy. Everything else depends on volume; we read 100% of messages and answer the ones where we can genuinely help.
Can you recommend a recorder for my situation?
We point you to the tools rather than playing email consultant: the Take the 60-second finder quiz matches devices to your workflow, and the buying guides cover the trade-offs. Buying questions with unusual constraints are fair game for the “general” topic, though.
I'm a manufacturer — how do we get covered or corrected?
Use the “partnership” or “correction” topics with sources. Coverage can't be bought, per our affiliate disclosure, but factual corrections from manufacturers get the same five-day verification treatment as anyone else's.
Can you help me with a recording dispute or legal question?
No — we can't answer legal questions. Our recording consent laws guide explains the framework and links the authoritative resources (Justia, RCFP, Digital Media Law Project); for anything with real consequences, please consult a lawyer.
What happens to the message I send?
It's delivered to our editorial inbox, seen only by editorial staff, used only to reply, and deleted on request — the whole arrangement is in our privacy policy, which is short enough to actually read.
