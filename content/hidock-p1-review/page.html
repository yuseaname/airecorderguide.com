The Earphone-Capture Difference

earphone-pairing two-way earphone recording changes the capture calculus compared to card-style or wearable recorders.

  • Near-field capture: Bluetooth earphones sit close to the mouth, delivering cleaner audio for diarization algorithms [SPEC].
  • Two-way capture: Unlike card-style recorders that may miss one side of a call, the earphone-pairing method captures both sides when earphones are paired during a call [SPEC].
  • Limitation: Range and crosstalk still degrade performance. In conference-room or far-field scenarios, accuracy drops off sharply — consistent with industry-wide diarization failure modes [SPEC, EDIT].

FAQ

Does HiDock P1 really capture both sides of a call?

Yes — the earphone-pairing two-way earphone recording method captures both sides of a call when the paired Bluetooth earphones are connected during a call. However, call quality for diarization depends on near-field capture; distance and crosstalk degrade label accuracy. This is an industry-wide diarization limitation, not unique to HiDock.

What is the warranty period for HiDock P1?

HiDock P1 carries an 18-month warranty for purchases made directly from hidock.com. For purchases through third-party retailers, warranty terms may differ. Confirm on the listing before purchase.

Does HiDock P1 work offline?

No. HiDock P1 requires an internet connection for AI transcription. It is not an offline/on-device transcription device.

What languages does HiDock P1 support?

HiDock P1 supports transcription in 75+ languages. This is a manufacturer‑stated figure; we have not independently verified coverage for each language.

Can I use HiDock P1 with any Bluetooth earphones?

HiDock P1 pairs with most Bluetooth earphones that support the HFP/HSP profiles for call audio. Some features (like two-way call capture) may depend on specific earphone models and firmware. Consult the HiDock P1 manual for compatibility details.

What is the 30-day trial period?

HiDock P1 includes a 30-day trial period from the date of delivery. If you are not satisfied, you can return the unit for a refund within 30 days, subject to the terms on hidock.com.

How does HiDock P1 compare to PLAUD Note for call recording?

HiDock P1 uses a earphone-pairing two-way earphone method for call capture, while PLAUD Note uses a built-in microphone array. HiDock P1 has no subscription fee ($169 one-time vs. $199 + $99.99/yr for PLAUD Note). PLAUD Note has published hardware specs; HiDock P1 does not publish storage, dimensions, or weight. Speaker diarization performance varies with environment; both require near-field capture for best results.
