The subscription question is the whole decision

Everything else is detail. HiDock’s model — pay once, transcribe as much as you want — removes the single biggest complaint in this category: subscription fatigue. Owner threads circle the same theme: "$20 a month" thresholds, minutes caps, sync anxiety. A device whose transcription is included simply exits that conversation.

The asterisk is longevity. An unlimited-included promise is only as durable as the company behind it, and the category has a cautionary tale: the Limitless Pendant, discontinued in late 2025 [unverified], stranding cloud-dependent features. Our no-subscription guide prices this risk honestly — you are trading a recurring fee for trust in a smaller company’s survival.

Where PLAUD Note still wins

The integration stack. Notion export, ChatGPT via MCP server, Zapier, Dropbox [SPEC] — if your notes feed an automated pipeline, PLAUD’s app is the category benchmark.

The documented record. The Note is the most independently reviewed device in the field — including its flaws (see our full review). HiDock’s independent coverage is thinner, which means fewer surprises documented and fewer verified.

Where HiDock P1 wins

Price certainty. $169, once, forever-as-promised. No minute counting, no tier math, no upgrade nagging.

Call capture without a plan. True call recording bundled with included transcription is rare at any price [SPEC].

The three-year arithmetic. $169 versus $428.97 is not a rounding error — it is a mid-range device’s entire cost. Run your own volume through the cost calculator and the gap only grows for heavy users.

Who each suits

  • HiDock P1: high-volume recorders, subscription refusers, and call-recording buyers who don’t want a monthly meter.
  • PLAUD Note: professionals whose notes feed Notion/ChatGPT pipelines, and buyers who prefer a documented, warts-and-all track record.

The dock difference, and other practical notes

Form follows the desk. HiDock’s dock-style design anchors it to workspaces in a way card-form devices are not — excellent for desk-bound professionals who record calls and meetings from a home base, less ideal for field capture that lives in a pocket. The form-factor guide frames this choice for every device in the category.

Verify the battery figure yourself. HiDock now publishes a 600 mAh battery on its own spec pages (hidock.com, retrieved Sep 2026) [SPEC] — a change from the undocumented state our earlier research flagged, and the figure the table above carries. Run-time claims still vary across listings, so re-check the manufacturer’s spec sheet before buying; this category’s numbers move quarter to quarter.

Cloud, still. Included transcription is not offline transcription: recordings upload for processing [SPEC], so the privacy posture matches PLAUD’s cloud pipeline rather than the local-first devices in our local vs cloud comparison. For client-confidential workflows, that shared architecture matters more than the billing model.

The consent map applies to both. Whichever you buy, recording law is unchanged — all-party consent states restrict recording regardless of hardware. Our state-by-state guide covers what both devices’ marketing pages do not.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is HiDock P1 really subscription-free?
Its core transcription is included with the device — unlimited, per manufacturer terms [SPEC], with optional packs for extras. The honest caveat: "included forever" is a promise about a company’s future, and this category has already buried a cloud-dependent device (the Limitless Pendant). Verify current terms on the manufacturer’s page before buying.
Which is cheaper over three years?
HiDock P1: about $169 total — hardware only. PLAUD Note: $428.97 once the 300 free minutes force the $99.99/yr Pro plan. The roughly $260 gap is the whole story of subscription math: full dataset here.
Does HiDock P1 record phone calls?
Yes — true call capture, not placement-only [SPEC], which is unusual among subscription-free devices and one of its strongest cards versus PLAUD alternatives.
Who should pick which?
HiDock for price certainty and call capture without a plan; PLAUD Note for the mature app, Notion/ChatGPT integrations, and 60-day standby. If wearable form matters more than either, start from the wearable guide.
